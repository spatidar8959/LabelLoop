Quick controls

Draw box: click and drag. 2-corner: click once for the first corner, then click the opposite corner. Double-click: add a quick 12%-size editable box. In Select mode, drag inside a box to move it or drag a larger corner/edge handle to resize.

Zoom and pan: scroll with the trackpad to move around the zoomed image. Hold ⌘ on Mac or Ctrl on Windows/Linux while scrolling to zoom at the pointer. You can also hold Space and drag to pan.

Keyboard: ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z undo, ⇧⌘Z / Ctrl+Y redo, Delete removes selected box, ← / → moves between images, D switches to draw, V switches to select.
